Microgravity Combustion Insights

In microgravity, flames become more spherical due to reduced buoyancy-driven flow. This changes heat transfer, oxygen mixing and soot production behaviour compared to Earth-based combustion.

Spacecraft Fire-Safety Relevance

Understanding flame dynamics in reduced gravity supports safer spacecraft design, emergency response planning and onboard material testing protocols.
