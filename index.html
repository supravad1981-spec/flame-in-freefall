07 / PROJECT IMPACT

WHY FLAME IN FREEFALL MATTERS

From experimental combustion data to safer human space exploration.

MISSION VALUE / 007
OUR MISSION

Turning combustion research into meaningful fire-safety insights.

Fire behaves differently in microgravity. By exploring available Flame Extinguishment Experiment (FLEX) measurements, this platform helps users investigate combustion behaviour, identify experimental patterns and understand why these findings matter for future spacecraft design.

01
◉

Understand Fire in Microgravity

Explore how fuel type, droplet diameter, burning rate and surrounding gas conditions relate to flame behaviour beyond Earth's gravity.

BENEFIT: BETTER SCIENTIFIC UNDERSTANDING
02
⌁

Compare Experimental Behaviour

Compare Methanol and Heptane measurements, investigate differences between experiments and examine individual test records instead of relying only on averages.

BENEFIT: EVIDENCE-BASED COMPARISON
03
⌕

Make Research More Accessible

Search, filter, visualize and inspect experimental records through one interface, making scientific data easier for students and researchers to explore.

BENEFIT: ACCESSIBLE RESEARCH EXPLORATION
04
◇

Improve Data Transparency

Preserve missing measurements, expose dataset limitations and allow users to inspect the available records rather than hiding incomplete information.

BENEFIT: MORE RESPONSIBLE DATA ANALYSIS
05
◎

Support Future Spacecraft Research

Help connect experimental observations with research questions about fire prevention, material selection and suppression strategies in crewed spacecraft.

BENEFIT: INFORMED ENGINEERING RESEARCH
06
✧

Prepare for Moon and Mars Missions

Make combustion research relevant to future lunar and Martian habitats by helping researchers investigate fire-related challenges in space exploration.

BENEFIT: FUTURE MISSION AWARENESS
THE BIGGER PICTURE

Safer exploration begins with better understanding.

Flame in Freefall transforms an available collection of experimental measurements into an interactive research experience — helping users explore the science behind combustion and its relevance to human spaceflight.
