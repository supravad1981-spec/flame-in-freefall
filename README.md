
# 🔥 Flame in Freefall
### AI-Powered Fire Safety Insights from Microgravity Combustion Data

## 🚀 Project Overview

**Flame in Freefall** is a data-driven project focused on understanding combustion and fire behaviour in microgravity environments, particularly in spacecraft.

Our project aims to explore NASA's microgravity combustion research and experimental datasets, analyze combustion-related parameters, and transform scientific data into meaningful insights through an interactive web-based dashboard.

By combining scientific research, Python-based data analysis, and interactive visualizations, we aim to make microgravity combustion data easier to understand and explore, while contributing to a better understanding of spacecraft fire-safety challenges.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Fire safety is a critical concern in human space exploration.

In microgravity environments:

- Flames can develop different shapes and structures.
- Buoyancy-driven natural convection is significantly reduced.
- Oxygen transport and combustion processes behave differently.
- Heat transfer and flame propagation can change.
- Fire detection and suppression present unique challenges for spacecraft.

Understanding these differences requires studying experimental combustion data and interpreting scientific observations.

Our project focuses on organizing, analyzing, and visualizing such information to provide useful insights into fire behaviour in space.

## 💡 Our Proposed Solution

We aim to develop an interactive, AI-powered dashboard that explores microgravity combustion experiments and presents scientific data in an understandable format.

The proposed platform will:

1. Explore relevant NASA microgravity combustion datasets.
2. Process and validate experimental data.
3. Analyze combustion-related variables and measurements.
4. Generate interactive graphs and visualizations.
5. Identify patterns, relationships, and potential anomalies in the data.
6. Present data-driven observations related to spacecraft fire safety.
